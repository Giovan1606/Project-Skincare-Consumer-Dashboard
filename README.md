# Skincare Consumer Insights Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive skincare consumer analysis dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel to understand consumer profiles, skin concerns, skincare behavior, purchasing channels, and brand preferences.

## Data Analysis

The project analyzes a dataset of 1,000 respondents and covers:

- Age group distribution
- Skin type and skin concern analysis
- Skin tone and dark spots
- Skincare routine and skin reaction
- Sunscreen usage and wrinkles/anti-aging
- Monthly skincare spending and routine level
- Purchase channel by age group
- Decision drivers and brand preference

## Dashboard

The interactive dashboard summarizes key consumer insights through visualizations and KPI cards, including:

- Total Respondents
- Most Common Skin Type
- Top Skin Concern
- Age Group vs Skin Concern
- Skin Type vs Skin Concern
- Routine Level vs Skin Reaction
- Monthly Spending vs Routine Level
- Purchase Channel vs Age Group
- Decision Driver vs Brand Preference

## Key Insights

- The largest respondent group is aged 18–24.
- Combination is the most common skin type.
- Dark spots are the most common primary skin concern.
- Skin concerns show different patterns across age groups.
- E-commerce is the most commonly used purchase channel, particularly among younger users.
- Skin compatibility is the most prominent factor in brand selection.
